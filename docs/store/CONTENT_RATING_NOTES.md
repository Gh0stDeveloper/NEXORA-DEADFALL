# Content rating questionnaire notes

These notes prepare the factual inputs for the store/IARC questionnaire. The final rating must come from the distribution platform questionnaire, not from this document.

## Violence

- Core gameplay is a shooter centered on fighting zombie/infected enemies.
- Firearms and melee-style zombie attacks are part of normal gameplay.
- Blood effects are present.
- Prepared dismemberment can remove head/arms/legs from infected enemies.
- Gore is functionally present across quality tiers, with lower visual budgets on lower tiers.
- No violence against real-world identifiable persons is designed into the current beta.

## Fear / horror

- Dark zombie outbreak setting.
- Infected enemies chase and attack the player.

## Online interaction

- Online rooms support up to four players.
- Player display names are visible in Squad sessions.
- Voice chat is not implemented in this beta.
- No free-form text chat is implemented in this beta.

## Purchases / gambling

- No in-app purchases in the current beta.
- No gambling, loot-box wagering or casino simulation in the current beta.

## Sexual content / nudity

- None designed into the current beta.

## Drugs / alcohol / tobacco

- No gameplay system centered on drugs, alcohol or tobacco in the current beta.

## User-generated content

- Players do not upload custom levels, images, videos or other UGC in the current beta.

Review these answers against the exact submitted build immediately before completing the store questionnaire.
