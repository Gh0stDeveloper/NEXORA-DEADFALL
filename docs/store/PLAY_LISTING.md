Survive infected hordes alone or with a four-player squad in a dark 3D shooter.

## Full description

NEXORA: DEADFALL is a mobile 3D zombie survival shooter built around fast combat, escalating hordes and cooperative survival.

Fight alone or join a room with up to four players. Coordinate through dangerous waves, revive downed squad members and reconnect to an interrupted multiplayer session. Weapons and damage are resolved by the authoritative game server in online play.

The Closed Beta includes:

- Offline Horde survival.
- Five infected archetypes: Walker, Runner, Crawler, Tank and Screamer.
- Body-zone damage and dismemberment/gore presentation.
- 1–4 player online Squad rooms.
- DOWNED, revive and bleedout gameplay.
- Campaign Mission 01: First Signal.
- Campaign Mission 02: Last Broadcast.
- Checkpoint recovery.
- Multiple mobile performance/quality tiers.

This is a pre-release beta. Content, progression, balance and server availability can change while testing continues.

Voice chat is not included in this build.
