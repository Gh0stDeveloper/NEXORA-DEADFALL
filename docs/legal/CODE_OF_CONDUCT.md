# NEXORA: DEADFALL Code of Conduct

Closed-beta multiplayer should remain usable for every invited tester.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other players and testers with respect.
- Use bugs and exploits only for controlled reproduction and reporting.
- Keep room codes/test credentials within the intended group.
- Report crashes, cheating and security problems accurately.
- Follow moderator/developer instructions during organized tests.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, threats or targeted abuse.
- Hate speech or discriminatory conduct.
- Cheating, packet/RPC abuse or deliberate service disruption.
- Impersonation or attempts to obtain another player's credentials/tokens.
- Malicious exploitation of crashes, reconnects or room capacity.
- Distribution of unauthorized modified clients as official beta builds.

## Enforcement

The developer may mute future communication features, remove a player from a room, revoke beta access or preserve relevant operational evidence when reasonably required to investigate abuse. Enforcement should be proportionate and limited to beta safety/security purposes.
