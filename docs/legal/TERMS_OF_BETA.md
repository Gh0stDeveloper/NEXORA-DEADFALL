Testers must satisfy the age and account requirements of the distribution platform and applicable law. Beta builds, room credentials and private testing links may not be redistributed outside the authorized testing group unless the developer permits it.

## Acceptable use

Do not exploit vulnerabilities, intentionally disrupt servers, automate abusive traffic, impersonate other players, harass other testers, distribute cheats or attempt unauthorized access to another user's session or data.

Good-faith security findings should be reported privately through the beta support channel and not weaponized against the service.

Multiplayer depends on Internet connectivity and beta server availability. The service may disconnect incompatible or abusive clients and may reset rooms or beta progression when necessary for testing.

## Content

The game contains zombie combat, blood and dismemberment/gore. Testers should review the distribution platform's content information before participating.

The beta is provided for testing and may contain defects. To the extent permitted by applicable law, no guarantee of uninterrupted operation, data preservation or future release is made. These beta terms may be updated as features or distribution requirements change.

## Enforcement

Access may be suspended or removed for abuse, cheating, harassment, credential redistribution or material violation of these terms or the Code of Conduct.
